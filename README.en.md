# Aev Battle

Aev Battle is a browser-based 3D arena battle game. The currently available mode is offline BOT battle. Online multiplayer, cloud saves, and persistent match records are not implemented.

## How to play

1. Choose a character and BOT difficulty, then select **戦闘開始** (Start Battle).
2. **PC:** Move with W/A/S/D or the arrow keys; hold Shift to sprint; use the mouse to look around; left-click or press Space to attack; press E for Skill and Q for Super.
3. **Phone/tablet:** Use the lower-left virtual stick to move, drag the right side of the screen to look around, and use the attack, SKILL, and SUPER buttons.
4. Defeat all enemy BOTs within the three-minute time limit to win. Reaching zero health or running out of time results in defeat.
5. On Hard and above, enemy projectiles home in on the player. On Impossible, enemy projectiles cause one-hit defeat and have stronger homing. Projectiles disappear when they hit obstacles.
6. Collecting gems restores health and increases the Super gauge.

Controls and gameplay rules may change between versions. Check the in-game rules panel for the current details.
